# Privacy

Flibbertigibbet Yourself runs entirely on your own machine.

- It reads only the spinner word Claude Code is about to draw and the turn's identifier, to choose a replacement phrase.
- It does not read the conversation, store or log anything, or send anything anywhere.
- It makes no network requests and collects no analytics or telemetry.

landrucode receives no data from the plugin.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/landrucode/flibbertigibbet-yourself/issues or email dev@landrucode.ai.
